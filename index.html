Data Usage & Privacy

How Your Story Is Used

Your submission is published in the form you provide it. Beyond publication, Mtaa Pulse aggregates submissions to identify patterns across regions, including recurring concerns, emerging improvements, and areas of community pride. It is this aggregated insight, not personal information, that informs our reporting and services.

Academic and research institutions access aggregated, ward-level trend data for study and analysis.
Investors and public officials may request the same category of aggregated trend data. Personal identifiers, including names, phone numbers, and precise coordinates, are never included or disclosed.
Phone numbers provided for follow-up are accessible only to authorized Mtaa Pulse personnel and are never shared, sold, or included in any external release.
